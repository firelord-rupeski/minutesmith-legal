[ ▸ ] How it works

From raw audio to finished minutes.

Four steps, played back like the meeting itself.

[00:00] Record

Capture the whole conversation.

Hit record — or press ⌘⇧R from anywhere — and Minutesmith grabs your microphone and the system audio from Zoom, Teams, or Meet. You get everyone's words, not just your half of the call.

Works in light or dark, and stays out of your way while you focus on the meeting.

[00:20] Transcribe

Transcribed on your Mac, even offline.

A local Whisper model turns speech into a clean, timestamped transcript using Metal GPU acceleration. Download a model once and it works offline from then on — no API key, no upload.

[00:45] Summarize

Summaries shaped to the meeting.

Standup, Weekly Sync, Sales Call, or General templates tailor each summary to the kind of conversation you had. Summarization runs on a local model by default — or plug in your own key when you want a specific one.

On-device OpenAI Anthropic Ollama

[01:10] Ask

Interrogate the transcript.

Wondering what was decided? Ask a plain-language question and get an answer drawn straight from what was actually said — with the transcript right beside it, so you can check the receipts.

Everything lives in a searchable local library of past meetings you can export as files whenever you need them.

[01:30] Your data

What's said in the room stays on your machine.

Recordings, transcripts, and summaries are saved as plain files in your own app-data folder — never uploaded to a server operated by us, because no such server exists. Turn on a cloud provider and the request goes straight from your Mac to them, using your key. We never sit in the middle.

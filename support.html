Reporting a bug

To help track down an issue quickly, please include:

  • Your macOS version
  • What you were doing when it happened (recording, transcribing, summarizing, exporting, etc.)
  • Whether you're using the built-in on-device model or a connected provider (OpenAI, Anthropic, Ollama)
  • Any error message shown in the app

Permissions

Minutesmith needs Microphone access to record your voice, and Screen Recording permission to capture system audio from meeting apps (audio only — no video or screen content is recorded). If a recording seems silent or incomplete, check System Settings → Privacy & Security to confirm both permissions are granted.
